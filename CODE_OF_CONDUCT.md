# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain a welcoming, respectful environment for contributors and visitors regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and respectfully.
- Offer useful feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted disclosure of private information, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you encounter concerning behavior, contact the repository maintainer privately through an appropriate GitHub contact channel. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation after reviewing reports fairly and confidentially where possible.

This policy applies to project spaces managed by the maintainer.
