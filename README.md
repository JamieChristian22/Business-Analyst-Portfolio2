## 🏥 Featured Case Study: Healthcare Claims Optimization

### Business Challenge

Healthcare claims workflows can experience processing delays, manual review bottlenecks, rework, and inconsistent operational visibility.

### Analytical Approach

1. Examine current-state claims processing.
2. Identify inefficiencies and potential root causes.
3. Define business and functional requirements.
4. Evaluate workflow automation opportunities.
5. Establish measurable performance indicators.
6. Document proposed process improvements.

### Case Study Performance Indicators

| Metric | Illustrative Result |
|---|---:|
| Processing Time Reduction | 55% |
| Rework Rate | 28% → 8% |
| Auto-Adjudication Rate | 78% |
| Modeled Annual Savings | $1.4M |

*Figures are reported within the portfolio case study and are not independently verified operational results.*

## 🔄 My Business Analysis Framework

```text
BUSINESS PROBLEM
       |
       v
STAKEHOLDER DISCOVERY
       |
       v
CURRENT-STATE ANALYSIS
       |
       v
REQUIREMENTS ENGINEERING
       |
       v
FUTURE-STATE SOLUTION DESIGN
       |
       v
VALIDATION & UAT
       |
       v
KPI ANALYSIS & RECOMMENDATIONS
       |
       v
BUSINESS VALUE
```

### Methodology

**1. Discover** — Understand business objectives, stakeholders, constraints, and pain points.

**2. Analyze** — Examine workflows, operational data, and performance gaps.

**3. Define** — Document requirements, acceptance criteria, and measurable success conditions.

**4. Design** — Model future-state workflows and recommend practical improvements.

**5. Validate** — Evaluate requirements coverage, testing outcomes, and solution alignment.

**6. Communicate** — Present insights, risks, trade-offs, and recommendations.
